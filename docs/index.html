Install the Zehnder ComfoAir controller firmware

For kits and DIY boards. Preprogrammed boards skip this page: just power them on and open the Home Assistant app.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop.
  2. Connect the board with a USB data cable.
  3. Press Install, pick the serial port, and keep the tab open.
  4. When asked, enter your Wi-Fi. The board then shows up in Home Assistant by itself.

Firmware …

Your browser can't talk to serial ports. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https.

Didn't get a Wi-Fi prompt?

Join the network zehnder-comfoair-xxxxxx from your phone; a setup page opens. The setup window closes ten minutes after power-on; unplug and replug to reopen it.

Afterwards

In the ESPHome dashboard, choose Adopt on the discovered device to get updates from this project. Source, pinout and changelog: github.com/JoooostB/esphome-zehnder-comfoair-rf.
